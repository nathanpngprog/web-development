Introduction

Welcome to the step-by-step tutorial on solving the Rubik's Cube using the beginner's method. This guide will walk you through each stage of the process, from creating the white cross to solving the last layer. Follow along and practice each step to become proficient in solving the cube.

Step 1: First Layer


White Cross

The cross is commonly started off on white and this tutorial uses white as the starting layer as well. The steps to solve the cross are enlisted below.

  • Locate any white edge on your cube and identify the non-white part of the edge (an edge has two colours, so a white edge would have a white part and a different coloured non-white part).
  • Match the non-white part to its own centre by hit and trial - this should be easy to do in 2-3 moves.
  • Once the piece has matched its centre, rotate the piece so that the white part matches the white centre as well.

You have successfully solved one piece! Now, repeat this process for the other white edges as well until you end up with a white cross.



White Corners

The white corners are the easiest to learn. Before moving on, try performing this sequence of moves on your cube - R U R’ U’. You have just learnt your very first algorithm! An algorithm is a series of moves that helps us move around pieces on the cube without disturbing the final positions of other pieces.

  • Start off by positioning your cube so that the white centre is at the bottom.
  • Locate a white corner in the top layer. Identify the non-white parts of the white corner.
  • Move the top layer until the white corner ends up between the corresponding centres of the non-white parts (refer to image).
  • Now, perform the algorithm R U R’ U’ until the white corner ends up in the bottom layer. You may have to perform this algorithm 1-3 times depending on the position of the white corner.
  • Repeat this process for the other white corners as well until you end up with a completely solved first layer.

Step 2: Second Layer


Non-yellow edges

Now that we have solved the first layer, we can move on to the second layer. The second layer consists of four non-yellow edges. The steps to solve the second layer are enlisted below.

  • Start off by positioning your cube so that the white centre is at the bottom.
  • Locate a non-yellow edge in the top layer. Identify the two non-yellow parts of the edge.
  • Move the top layer until the non-yellow edge ends up between the corresponding centres of the non-yellow parts (refer to image).
  • Now, perform the algorithm U R U’ R’ U’ F’ U F if the edge needs to go to the right or U’ L’ U L U F U’ F’ if it needs to go to the left. You may have to perform this algorithm 1-3 times depending on the position of the edge.
  • Repeat this process for the other non-yellow edges as well until you end up with a completely solved second layer.

Step 3: Last Layer

Yellow Cross

Now that we have solved the first two layers, we can move on to the last layer. The first step in solving the last layer is to create a yellow cross. The steps to create a yellow cross are enlisted below.

  • Start off by positioning your cube so that the white centre is at the bottom.
  • Identify the current state of the yellow pieces in the top layer. There are three possible states - dot, L-shape and line.
  • If you have a dot, perform the algorithm F R U R’ U’ F’ twice. If you have an L-shape, position it so that it looks like a backward L and perform the algorithm F R U R’ U’ F’ once. If you have a line, position it so that it is horizontal and perform the algorithm F R U R’ U’ F’ once.

Now to match all the corner pieces in the top layer, find a corner that is already matched and keep it towards the front-right of the cube. If none of the corner pieces is in the right place, you can hold the cube in any orientation with the unmatched pieces on top and apply the algorithm.



Last Layer Corners

Top Layer Corners

Now, keeping the yellow side on top and the corner which is not aligned on the front right corner, use the algorithm R’ D’ R D till the corner is aligned, then rotate the top layer and bring the unaligned corner to the front right corner and then again apply the same algorithm till the corner is solved. Repeat the process until all the corners are solved.

Congratulations! You have successfully solved the Rubik's Cube using the beginner's method. With practice, you can improve your speed and efficiency in solving the cube. Keep practicing and enjoy the challenge!
