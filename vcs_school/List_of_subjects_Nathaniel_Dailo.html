  1. Lesson 2: Understanding Motion

Translational Motion is the movement of an object in a straight line, Rotational Motion is the movement of an object around a central axis, Oscillatory Motion is the back-and-forth movement of an object around a central point, Linear Displacement is the change in position of an object along a straight line, Linear velocity is the rate of change of linear displacement, and Linear acceleration is the rate of change of linear velocity
    2. To learn about the different types of motion and their characteristics
    3. I can use it to understand and apply concepts related to motion.
  1. Lesson 3: Newton's Laws of Motion
    1. Newton's First Law states that an object at rest stays at rest and an object in motion stays in motion with the same speed and in the same direction unless acted upon by an unbalanced force, Newton's Second Law states that the acceleration of an object as produced by a net force is directly proportional to the magnitude of the net force, in the same direction as the net force, and inversely proportional to the mass of the object, and Newton's Third Law states that for every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.
    2. To understand the relationship between force and motion
    3. I can use it to analyze and predict the behavior of objects under the influence of forces.
